Profile

Recorded description

Elaine keeps the cabinet's index, and she is the only archivist who writes down what the index leaves out. She arrives at the Fifth Cabinet before dawn, when the reading room is still cold and the catalogue drawers are easy to open.

Her notes on Maren Kessler are careful, but they never mention the sealed drawer in the western stacks. Tobias Vale says that omission is the closest thing to a confession she has ever written.
